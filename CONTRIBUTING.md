## Morrowind Screenshots

The goal of the project is to seek out the hidden beauty and celebrate the visual aesthetics of [Morrowind](#morrowind).
Project authors capture them by making ingame [screenshots and videos or drawings](#post-types), which are then
[reviewed by the editor](#editing) and pearled off for [publication](#publication) in all project's accounts. There are
some [core principles](#core-principles) that the project follows. Anyone is welcome to
[contribute to the project](#contributing). The code and content are distributed under open [licenses](#license).

[Instagram](https://instagram.com/mwscr/)  
[VK](https://vk.com/mwscr)  
[Telegram](https://t.me/mwscr)  
[MAX](https://max.ru/channel_mwscr)  
[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@mwscr)  
[GitHub](https://github.com/dehero/mwscr)

### Posts

Main section of [Morrowind Screenshots](#mwscr) project, which includes only [post types](#post-types) that follow the
project's [core principles](#core-principles). [Strict rules](#violations) are applied for all content in this section.

[Go to Posts](https://mwscr.dehero.site/posts/)

### Extras

Secondary section of [Morrowind Screenshots](#mwscr) project, which includes various extra [post types](#post-types)
that can ignore some of [violations](#violations) or for which they cannot be applied.

[Go to Extras](https://mwscr.dehero.site/extras/)

### Drafts

A place where all incoming [screenshots, videos, drawings](#proposal) and [post requests](#request) are stored. Everyone
can see what was proposed and what's going to be posted. [The editing](#editing) is made on Drafts content. Posts for
[publication](#publication) are selected from Drafts by the [post selection](#post-selection) rules. If the files of a
draft are removed from the storage, the draft is automatically rejected and moved to [Rejects](#trash).

[Go to Drafts](https://mwscr.dehero.site/drafts/)

### Rejects

Stores all posts and post requests that are not going to be published or fulfilled. Serves as a showcase of
[ordinary screenshots](#post-mark), [violations](#violations) and locations that could be revisited to make better
shots.

[Go to Rejects](https://mwscr.dehero.site/rejects/)

<a id="core-principles"></a>

## Core principles

The [Morrowind Screenshots](#mwscr) project follows the core principles, which are not going to be changed ever. No
[graphic and unlore mods](#no-mods) are welcome. No [processing](#no-processing) should be used. No
[user interface](#no-ui) should be visible.

To expect your work [to be accepted](#proposal) into the [Posts](#posts) section, described strict requirements must be
met and other possible [violations](#violations) should be avoided. Otherwise, you can consider getting into
[Extras](#extras) section which is more democratic.

<a id="no-mods"></a>

### No modifications

There should be no content or [graphical modifications](#vanilla-look) to the game, except for
[allowed modifications](#allowed-mods). Your screenshot will not be considered for [Posts](#posts) section if there are
textures, models, objects, or situations that could not have been in the original game with addons.

> **Possible Violation:** Uses or requires mods

<a id="no-processing"></a>

### No processing

Screenshots and videos that have been processed in a graphic editor beforehand will not be accepted.
[Minor processing](#allowed-processing) is allowed only during [the editing process](#editing). If you still want to
offer your own variant of processing, be sure to send the original image as well.

> **Possible Violation:** Graphic filters

<a id="no-ui"></a>

### No interface elements

For [Posts](#posts) section all interface elements (crosshair, map, health bars, menu, console, etc.) should be hidden
from the screen. You can disable the interface with the console command `tm` or `ToggleMenus`, or in the
[OpenMW](#openmw) engine with the `F11` key.

> **Possible Violation:** UI is visible

<a id="shot"></a>

### Shot

A square or rectangular screenshot, the most popular [type of posts](#post-types) in [Morrowind Screenshots](#mwscr).

[Go to Shots](https://mwscr.dehero.site/posts/?type=shot)

<a id="compilation"></a>

### Compilation

A set made of 4 previously published posts of [other types](#post-types) from the [main section](#posts), united by a
common subject or idea. All items must share the same [aspect ratio](#aspect-ratio).

[Go to Compilations](https://mwscr.dehero.site/posts/?type=compilation)

<a id="wallpaper"></a>

### Wallpaper

A screenshot composed to work as a desktop or smartphone wallpaper, in both horizontal and vertical orientations.
Horizontal wallpapers and [Shots](#shot) with a horizontal orientation have [different aspect ratios](#post-types).

[Go to Wallpapers](https://mwscr.dehero.site/posts/?type=wallpaper)

<a id="clip"></a>

### Clip

A short video of up to 1 minute, square or vertical, sized for social feeds. It is used for small self-contained moments
from the [game world](#morrowind) as the video analogue of [static shots](#shot). Longer and horizontal videos belong to
[Video](#video).

[Go to Clips](https://mwscr.dehero.site/posts/?type=clip)

<a id="video"></a>

### Video

A long-form horizontal video of more than 1 minute, containing beautiful or interesting footage of the
[game world](#morrowind). It is used where a short [Clip](#clip) is not enough. Like screenshots, videos must follow the
[core principles of the project](#core-principles), including not containing [UI elements](#no-ui). The
[format and quality of the video file](#file-format) are also important.

[Go to Videos](https://mwscr.dehero.site/posts/?type=video)

<a id="redrawing"></a>

### Redrawing

A redrawing [post type](#post-types) must be based on a previously published [Shot](#shot) and include it as a second
image for comparison, with a credit to [the original author](#post-author).

[Go to Redrawings](https://mwscr.dehero.site/extras/?type=redrawing)

<a id="outtakes"></a>

### Outtakes

A set of 10 interesting screenshots or videos from [Morrowind](#morrowind) that violate the
[strict rules of the project](#violations), submitted by [project members](#members). It is the most democratic
[post type](#post-types), easy to get into by simply [uploading your screenshots to the project](#proposal).

[Go to Outtakes](https://mwscr.dehero.site/extras/?type=outtakes)

<a id="news"></a>

### News

A [post type](#post-types) with project news. Must contain the news text in the [description](#post-description) and may
include an optional illustration.

[Go to News](https://mwscr.dehero.site/extras/?type=news)

<a id="photoshop"></a>

### Photoshop

A photomontage based on a previously published [Shot](#shot). This [post type](#post-types), like
[Redrawing](#redrawing), includes the original shot as a second image, for comparison.

[Go to Photoshops](https://mwscr.dehero.site/extras/?type=photoshop)

<a id="mention"></a>

### Mention

A link to the project on another site, a repost, or the use of [Morrowind Screenshots](#mwscr) materials in posts by
other projects and accounts. Not every mention falls into [this category](#post-types) - only those that stand out from
the general background and are known to the [project administrator](#admin). A post of this type should contain a
screenshot or video of the mention in [snapshot](#post-media) and an optional link to it in the
[description](#post-description).

[Go to Mentions](https://mwscr.dehero.site/extras/?type=mention)

<a id="merch"></a>

### Merch

An item that can be ordered to support the [Morrowind Screenshots](#mwscr) project. Posts of [this type](#post-types)
must have a [description](#post-description), as well as one or more images of the merch itself. On the site such a post
has an "Order" button that opens the order dialog. As a rule, the appearance of merch is accompanied by a post of type
[News](#news). Unlike other post types, which are published to the project's social media, merch
[is published](#publication) in services that allow ordering and payment, such as Boosty, Avito and others.

[Go to Merch](https://mwscr.dehero.site/extras/?type=merch)

<a id="achievement"></a>

### Achievement

A [post type](#post-types) for notable events, records and prize places in the project or in the game, achieved by the
project itself or by its members. Such a post should include one or more images as confirmation.

[Go to Achievements](https://mwscr.dehero.site/extras/?type=achievement)

## Contributing

To contribute to the project you can [submit screenshots, videos or drawings](#proposal) for publication,
[create a compilation](#creating-compilation) from published screenshots, [request a screenshot or video](#request) by
describing what you would like to see, [find missing location](#suggesting-location) for posts without location or
[improve the repository](#improve-repository) on GitHub.

<a id="proposal"></a>

### Propose work

Before you can propose your screenshots, videos or drawings, read the [core principles](#core-principles) and
[shooting tips](#shooting-tips). Then send your work in any convenient way. Provide your profile addresses if you want a
link to you in the post when the work is published. Proposed works will be automatically checked and will go to
[Drafts](#drafts). After [editing](#editing), your works will be [published](#publication) or [rejected](#trash). By
submitting your work, you agree that it will be stored in the project archive and may be used in any sources related to
the project, with attribution to you.

[Upload works on the project website](https://mwscr.dehero.site/#post-proposal)  
[Send to Telegram bot](https://t.me/mwscrbot)  
[Send proposal via GitHub Issues](https://github.com/dehero/mwscr/issues/new?labels=post-proposal&template=post-proposal.yml) (check
out
[file size restrictions](https://docs.github.com/en/get-started/writing-on-github/working-with-advanced-formatting/attaching-files))  
[Send to administrator via email](mailto:me@dehero.site?subject=mwscr)

<a id="creating-compilation"></a>

### Create compilation

Right click (long tap) on published post to show the context menu. Click "Select" button to start selecting posts, then
select posts you want to compile together. Use filters and sorting to find posts in the list.

After selecting all needed posts click "Compile Selected" on the toolbar to create compilation draft. You need 4
screenshots to create [Compilation](#compilation) that can be published.

<a id="request"></a>

### Request a screenshot or video

To ask the authors of the project to make a certain screenshot or video, leave a free-form message in any convenient
way. The easiest way is to write a comment or private message to the administrator in any of the project accounts.
Another option is to use [the dialog](https://mwscr.dehero.site/#post-request/drafts) on the project website to then
submit your request as a patch. Your request will be added to [Drafts](#drafts) and will remain there, waiting for the
appropriate material. The request may be rejected if it does not meet the [core principles](#core-principles).

<a id="suggesting-location"></a>

### Find missing location

If the [shooting location](#post-location) of the screenshot or video is not specified in the post, you can suggest one.
Navigate to post page or right click (long tap) on post to show the context menu. Click "Locate" button and choose
location from the list in the dialog. You can select multiple locations if needed.

Submit your suggestion using any of available submitting options in the dialog. Another option is to write a comment
with the suggested location under post in any of [project's channels](#mwscr). The [project administrator](#admin) will
check your suggestion and accept it if it was confirmed.

## Members

Information about project members is stored in the
[members list](https://github.com/dehero/mwscr/blob/main/data/users.yml). For each member the project keeps a display
name, an avatar, an optional Russian spelling of the name, and links to profiles in the [project accounts](#mwscr). A

<a id="shooting-tips"></a>

## Shooting tips

To make it easier to take high-quality pictures, use appropriate
[game engine and utilities](#game-engine-and-utilities), select lossless [file format](#file-format), keep in mind
[aspect ratio](#aspect-ratio), adjust [field of view](#fov) and [time of day](#gamehour) on the fly, control the
[drawing distance and fog](#distance), always use [free camera](#free-camera) and [god mode](#god-mode), try to
[move quickly](#player-speed) and feel free to [quick travel](#quick-travel).

Also, remember about [auxilary plugins](#allowed-mods) that make the shooting process easier.

<a id="game-engine-and-utilities"></a>

### Game engine and utilities

Preferably use the latest version of the [OpenMW](#openmw) engine or the vanilla engine with [MGE](#mge). You need to
set high resolution and enable anti-aliasing. You can enable slight graphical enhancements, such as water shaders. You
can increase the number of loadable cells in exteriors, but using distant land is not recommended. To take screenshots
in the vanilla engine, use the `PrtScn` key, and in the OpenMW engine, use the `F12` key. To capture video, you can use
screen capture programs such as OBS Studio. In a post, the engine used for the capture is specified: OpenMW or Vanilla.

<a id="openmw"></a>

#### OpenMW

A free and open-source [game engine](#game-engine-and-utilities) recreation for [Morrowind](#morrowind), which aims to
address issues with the vanilla engine that no longer has support or bug-fix updates. Used for the most of the project's
content.

[OpenMW Site](https://openmw.org/)

<a id="mge"></a>

#### Morrowind Graphics Extender

A graphics improvement add-on to the [vanilla engine](#game-engine-and-utilities), for longer viewing distances, great
sunsets, fine shaders and better lighting. This is not compatible with [OpenMW](#openmw).

[Download MGE](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/41102)

<a id="aspect-ratio"></a>

### Aspect ratio

Keep in mind that your screenshot or video [may be cropped](#allowed-processing) before the publication, so all the
important objects should stay in the final format and the composition should remain balanced. To simplify things, you
can purposely launch the game in a window with the desired aspect ratio. A common scenario is to run the game in a
window with resolution 1080x1080, which is the most common image size for [Shots](#shot).

Allowed aspect ratios with recommended and minimum dimentions are:

| Aspect ratio | Recommended |  Minimum  |
| ------------ | :---------: | :-------: |
| 16:9         |  1920x1080  | 1920x1080 |
| 3:2          |  1620x1080  | 1200x800  |
| 1:1          |  1080x1080  |  800x800  |
| 9:16         |  1080x1920  | 1080x1920 |
| 9:19.5       |  1080x2340  | 1080x2340 |

<a id="fov"></a>

### Field of view

You can change the field of view in the game settings on the fly, increasing it to get more objects in the frame or,
vice versa, decreasing it to get a close-up. Be sure to do so within reasonable limits and avoid distorting the
perspective too much.

<a id="distance"></a>

### Viewing distance and fog

Control the viewing distance in the game settings, depending on your artistic intent. Sometimes it's better to cover the
distant background with haze to keep the viewer's attention on the main foreground objects. For shooting distant
backgrounds, you can slightly increase the drawing distance. Make sure that the look of the game remains close to
vanilla.

<a id="free-camera"></a>

### Free camera

The console command `tcl` or `ToggleCollision` enables the free camera mode, in which you can levitate freely and walk
through walls. This helps you get interesting shots from hard-to-reach angles.

<a id="god-mode"></a>

### God mode

The `tgm` or `ToggleGodMode` console command protects you from falling damage and character attacks, allowing you to
concentrate on shooting without worrying about the protagonist's health. The [NoFightNoHello](#nofightnohello) plugin
can also protect you from character attacks, but it will be harder to shoot battle scenes with it.

<a id="quick-travel"></a>

### Quick travel

To travel to an exterior without a name, type its coordinates in the console:

```
coe 10 -5
```

To travel to a named exterior or to an interior, type its name:

```
coc "Ald-ruhn, Manor District"
```

<a id="player-speed"></a>

### Movement speed

To speed up your character a lot, type in the console:

```
player->setspeed 500
```

You can set higher values. This helps you move around the world faster while looking for shooting locations. Once you
find a shooting location, you'll want to get a more accurate angle by slowing down your movement. To make your character
as slow as possible, type:

```
player->setspeed 0
```

<a id="gamehour"></a>

### Time of day

When shooting exteriors, it can be useful to adjust the lighting or moon position by changing the time of day. For
example, to shoot during sunset at 7:00 PM, set the hour in the console:

```
set gamehour to 19
```

<a id="allowed-mods"></a>

## Allowed modifications

Official expansions Tribunal and Bloodmoon from Bethesda Softworks and the white list of unofficial expansions, which
currently includes only [Tamriel Rebuilt](#tamriel-rebuilt), are allowed. Plugins that give you additional options when
shooting but don't change the game visually such as [NoFightNoHello](#nofightnohello),
[MannequinChallenge](#mannequinchallenge) and [TrackPath](#trackpath) are allowed also.

In a post, the addon from the white list explicitly involved is specified.

<a id="tamriel-rebuilt"></a>

### Tamriel Rebuilt

The well-known unofficial expansion by Tamriel Rebuilt Project team that adds the mainland territory to
[Morrowind](#morrowind).

[Tamriel Rebuilt Site](https://www.tamriel-rebuilt.org/)

<a id="nofightnohello"></a>

### NoFightNoHello

[A plugin](#allowed-mods) that turns off greeting phrases and removes aggression from all NPCs and creatures. Allows you
to take screenshots without distracting from gameplay.

[Download NoFightNoHello](https://mwscr.dehero.site/plugins/NoFightNoHello.esp)

<a id="mannequinchallenge"></a>

### MannequinChallenge

[A plugin](#allowed-mods) that applies a permanent paralysis effect to an NPC or creature. Allows you to lock a
character in one place to make shooting easier. To apply the effect, you need to open the console, select the character,
and type:

```
addspell mannequinchallenge
```

To remove the effect type:

```
removespell mannequinchallenge
```

[Download MannequinChallenge](https://mwscr.dehero.site/plugins/MannequinChallenge.esp)

<a id="trackpath"></a>

### TrackPath

[A plugin](#allowed-mods) that allows you to record the player's movement along three coordinates and play it back
smoothly later. Suitable for preparing smooth movement through the game world when shooting video. Works well only on
the [OpenMW](#openmw) engine.

To start recording the player's movement, open the console and type:

```
set trackPath to 1
```

A message will appear that the recording of path `PATH_NUMBER` has started. You need to memorize this number to be able
to play the recorded path later. Move your character around the game world. You can move between locations by activating
doors; the recording of movement will continue. To stop the recording of the player's movement, type in the console:

```
set trackPath to 0
```

Before playing the recorded path, you need to move the character to the original location, where the beginning of the
path is located. To play the recorded path `PATH_NUMBER`, type in the console:

```
set playPath to PATH_NUMBER
```

The path will start playing, smoothly moving your character through the world. The transition between locations is not
automatic. You need to manually activate the doors when you are close to them to continue playing the path. You can
interrupt playing the path with the command:

```
set playPath to 0
```

[Download TrackPath](https://mwscr.dehero.site/plugins/TrackPath.esp)

<a id="locations"></a>

## Locations

The project contains a list of all game locations from the original [Morrowind](#morrowind) and the official
[Tribunal or Bloodmoon](#allowed-mods) addons. It is used to specify the [shooting location](#post-location) of posts,
to build the location filter, and to draw the [world map](#world-map). Each location in the list has an English title
and a Russian translation, the addon it belongs to, and one of the types:

Interior — an indoor area, such as a cave, tomb or building interior.  
Exterior — a named outdoor place, such as a city, camp or landmark.  
Region — a large outdoor area that unites many cells, such as the
[Ashlands Region](https://mwscr.dehero.site/posts/?location=Ashlands+Region&original=true).  
Group — a location that has no game resource of its own, but is structurally implied in the location tree, such as
[Old Mournhold](https://mwscr.dehero.site/posts/?location=Old+Mournhold&original=true).

Exteriors and regions can occupy one or several cells of the [world map](#world-map).

<a id="world-map"></a>

### World map

The project site has a world map used to view and select [shooting locations](#post-location) on post pages and in the
location filter. The map covers the base game and the [Bloodmoon](#allowed-mods) addon, the islands of Vvardenfell and
Solstheim. The [Tribunal](#allowed-mods) addon has no additional locations on the world map. Locations of the unofficial
[Tamriel Rebuilt](#tamriel-rebuilt) addon are deliberately not included in the project.

The map is based on the original game map of [Morrowind](#morrowind).

<a id="editing"></a>

## Editing

The process of selecting and preparing the posts for publication. Editing is performed by the project administrator and
includes search for [violations](#violations), [merging posts](#merging-posts),
[allowed processing](#allowed-processing) and [preparing a post](#preparing-post). You can follow the editing process by
observing [Drafts](#drafts).

Drafts are stored in full size, and access to them requires the editor's key set in the site settings.

<a id="violations"></a>

### Violations

The content of the post is checked automatically and manually, and if any violations are found, the post is moved to
[Rejects](#trash). Common violations include [inappropriate content](#appropriate-content), [unsupported](#file-format)
and [unreachable](#available-resource) resource.

If you expect your post to be published in [Posts](#posts) section, follow [core principles](#core-principles) and avoid
other violations like [non-vanilla look](#vanilla-look), [no anti-aliasing](#anti-aliasing),
[JPEG artifacts](#no-jpeg-artifacts), [graphic issues](#no-graphic-issues).

<a id="appropriate-content"></a>

#### Appropriate content

Original, previously unpublished screenshots and videos captured in [Morrowind](#morrowind) game, as well as redrawings
of previously published posts, photos, photomontages, out-of-game screenshots, and other materials related to the
project or the game are considered acceptable content. When talking about [post requests](#request) they should be
written clearly and don't contain vulgar language.

> **Possible Violation:** Inappropriate content

<a id="file-format"></a>

#### File format

The project supports the following file formats:

| Type              | Formats         | Max size | Notes                                                    |
| ----------------- | --------------- | :------: | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Images (lossless) | PNG, BMP        |  10 MB   |                                                          |
| Images (lossy)    | JPEG, WEBP, GIF |   5 MB   |                                                          |
| Video             | MP4, AVI        |  200 MB  | Min duration 10 sec, min framerate 30 fps, high bitrate. |
| Archive           | ZIP             |  100 MB  |                                                          |

Files can also be folded into a "ZIP" archive for easy transfer. The maximum size may be lower depending on the
submission method.

> **Possible Violation:** Unsupported resource

<a id="available-resource"></a>

#### Available resource

Morrowind Screenshots project can operate different kind of resource URL's. `store:/` links lead to files stored in
project's own private storage. `uploads:/` links lead to files uploaded through the project website; such files are
temporary and expire after about a week, unless a saved draft or patch references them. `http://` and `https://` links
lead to external resources.

When adding links manually always check the link to have no mistypes and for being accessible without authorization.

> **Possible Violation:** Unreachable resource

<a id="vanilla-look"></a>

#### Vanilla look

Too far [viewing distance](#distance), Distant Land, eye-catching shadows, color shaders, and other visual effects that
are at odds with [Morrowind](#morrowind)'s original aesthetic should be avoided when shooting footage for
[Posts](#posts) section.

Remember that mysterious fog, an understated color scheme, and strange angular shapes are important parts of the
atmosphere of our favorite game. Your goal is not to demonstrate the possibilities of mods but to show how beautiful the
game can be without them.

> **Possible Violation:** Non-vanilla look

<a id="anti-aliasing"></a>

#### Anti-aliasing

Screenshots and videos for [Posts](#posts) section should be captured with anti-aliasing option enabled. If you use
[OpenMW](#openmw), open the Launcher, go to "Graphics" section of and set "Anti-aliasing" to 8 on "Display" tab. If you
use vanilla engine with [MGE](#mge), go to "Global graphics" tab, find "Scene" section and set "Antialiasing level" to
8x.

> **Possible Violation:** No anti-aliasing

<a id="no-jpeg-artifacts"></a>

#### No JPEG artifacts

Screenshots for [Posts](#posts) section should be initially captured in "PNG" or "BMP" graphic format (uncompressed). It
is not allowed to convert images originally saved as "JPEG" to "PNG" to circumvent this restriction. Compression
artifacts remain visible in the image and spoil the purity of the original screenshot.

> **Possible Violation:** JPEG artifacts

<a id="no-graphic-issues"></a>

#### No graphic issues

When shooting for [Posts](#posts) section you should avoid screenshots and videos where the player is outside the game
world, cut edges of models are visible, some models overlap with others, or other graphical bugs and imperfections are
present.

> **Possible Violation:** Graphic issues

<a id="merging-posts"></a>

### Merging posts

Multiple variants of the same work in [Drafts](#drafts) are merged into a single post to make it easier to choose which
variant to publish. If the work is a response to one of the pending [requests](#request), it is merged with the original
request into a single post.

Right click (long tap) on a post to show the context menu. Click "Select" button to start selecting posts, then select
the posts you want to merge. Click "Merge Selected" on the toolbar to merge selected posts into a single post.

<a id="allowed-processing"></a>

### Allowed processing

Content added to [Drafts](#drafts) can be processed with the graphics editor during [the editing process](#editing).
Processing is intended to improve the perception of the original work while preserving the original content as much as
possible. It may include cropping, brightness adjustment, correction of technical errors in shooting (in rare cases),
duration trimming (for video). It is forbidden to change the color scheme, apply filters, add, change, or distort
objects. During processing, the original files remain intact, and each new variant is saved to a separate file with the
variant number appended to the file name, as described in the [post identifier](#post-id). For example:

```txt
dehero.2024-02-17-10-53-36.png
dehero.2024-02-17-10-53-36.1.png
dehero.2024-02-17-10-53-36.2.png
```

<a id="preparing-post"></a>

### Preparing a post

Preparing a post for publication includes selecting [type](#post-types) and [media](#post-media), [title](#post-title)
and [description](#post-description), [author](#post-author), filling in [tags](#post-tags),
[game engine](#game-engine-and-utilities), [addon](#allowed-mods), shooting [location](#post-location) and
[placement](#post-placement), and selecting [editor's mark](#post-mark). The post [identifier](#post-id) is assigned
automatically.

<a id="post-media"></a>

#### Post media

A post stores its files in three fields.

Content is the main media of a post: the screenshot, video, drawing or set of images it is about. Content files are
cropped to the post [aspect ratio](#aspect-ratio) when it is set and are shown in full when it is not. It is mostly the
content that is used for [publications](#publication).

Snapshot is a screenshot (not from [the game](#morrowind)) or other photographic evidence that serves as the basis for a
post. It is always shown in full size with scrolling. For [Mentions](#mention) it is the main media, since this post
type has no content. For other types this field is optional and can be used when necessary.

Trash stores files rejected during the [editing](#editing) of a post. Their previews are kept in the project for
history, but they are not counted in any way, not [published](#publication) and not shown anywhere.

<a id="post-title"></a>

#### Post title

The title of the post is chosen according to its content and can either simply describe what is shown or expand the
perception of the post by using analogies, references, and other techniques. The title suggested by the author of the
work is also taken into account. The title should be filled in English and translated into Russian.

English titles are formatted automatically: only Latin letters, digits and the punctuation marks `' , - . : + " ! ?` are
kept, and a fixed list of short words, such as "a", "of" or "the", is lowercased.

<a id="post-description"></a>

#### Post description

An optional text field of a post that supplements the [title](#post-title) and gives additional context. The description
is filled in English and translated into Russian. It uses Markdown syntax, so links can be inserted into it.

```
Learn [Markdown syntax](https://www.markdownguide.org/basic-syntax/)
```

The description is shown in the published post and is taken into account when searching posts. For [News](#news) posts
the description is required and contains the news text.

<a id="post-author"></a>

#### Post author

The project contributor who proposed the original work for publication. For [Redrawing](#redrawing) type posts, the
first author is the author of the drawing, and the second author is the author of the original post. For
[Compilation](#compilation) posts, the authors of all images in the set are listed. Each of these authors must be added
to the [members list](#members) beforehand. A member listed as the author of a published work receives the
[author](#author) role.

<a id="post-tags"></a>

#### Post tags

Post tags are filled in based on the content of the post in Latin letters separated by a space. Posts with similar
content should receive similar tags to make it easier to find posts on the same subject. Information that is specified
in other fields of the post should be avoided in tags.

<a id="post-location"></a>

#### Post location

Specifies the game location that is represented in the post according to the [location list](#locations). The goal is to
provide a practical landmark for finding the place in the game; the exact position within that place is not required.
For exterior shots, specify both the region and, when possible, the object shown or the nearby landmark, such as a
shipwreck or a plantation. Additional details, such as an exterior cell number, can be added in the note when selecting
a location. Specifying only the region may not be sufficient: it may later be difficult to return to the shot and
determine the location more precisely. If no location can be determined, [it can be suggested](#suggesting-location)
later.

<a id="post-placement"></a>

#### Post placement

Describes where the content of the post was captured: indoors, outdoors or mixed (for compilations). The placement is
set when [preparing a post](#preparing-post) and is taken into account when searching and filtering posts.

<a id="post-mark"></a>

#### Editor's mark

The editor's mark helps to filter [Drafts](#drafts) and monitor the overall quality of the project's post feed
[during editing](#editing).

| Mark   | Description                                                                                |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| A1, A2 | First-class posts, the most beautiful and artistic                                         |
| B1, B2 | More ordinary posts, but still good enough                                                 |
| C      | Rare not very good but publishable posts                                                   |
| E      | Experimental posts, which seem interesting but without certainty                           |
| D      | Too ordinary or doubling posts, which should be deleted to [Rejects](#trash)               |
| F      | Failed post attempts, candidates for reshooting or revisiting, stored in [Rejects](#trash) |

The mark is selected by the editor by the following indicators:

| Indicator   | A1  | A2  | B1  | B2  |  C  |  D  |  E  |  F  | Evaluated                               |
| ----------- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | --------------------------------------- |
| Artistry    |  +  |  +  |  ±  |     |     |     |  ±  |     | Beauty, intent.                         |
| Neatness    |  +  |  +  |  ±  |  +  |     |     |     |     | Graphics, framing.                      |
| Composition |  +  |  ±  |  +  |  ±  |  ±  |     |  ±  |     | Foreshortening, placement of objects.   |
| Lighting    |  +  |  ±  |  +  |  ±  |  ±  |     |  ±  |     | Light and colors.                       |
| Experiment  |     |     |     |     |     |     |  +  |     | Unconventional view.                    |
| Fail        |     |     |     |     |     |     |     |  +  | Potential for reshooting or revisiting. |
| Publishable |  ✓  |  ✓  |  ✓  |  ✓  |  ✓  |     |  ✓  |     |                                         |

A work receives the mark of a column if all indicators marked + are filled in and at least one indicator marked ± is
filled in.

Keep in mind that the mark is subjective and there is a more objective [post rating](#post-rating) score that evaluates
published posts by subscribers reactions.

<a id="post-id"></a>

### Post identifier

Every post has an identifierthat also defines the names of its files in the storage.

A published post id looks like `YYYY-MM-DD-N-title`, where the date is the publication date, N is the order number
within that day (no more than 9 posts per day), and the title is a shortened Latin title.

A draft id looks like `author.YYYY-MM-DD-title`, where author is the identifier of the member who proposed the work and
the date is the date of draft creation.

<a id="publication"></a>

## Publication

[Drafts](#drafts) posts that have successfully passed [editing](#editing) are placed to the shortlist, from where they
are gradually taken to create new posts. A new post is selected every day according to the
[post selection](#post-selection) rules. The post is published simultaneously on all project accounts that support this
type of post. Previously published posts may be published again, without changes, or as part of a different
[post type](#post-types).

The text of the publication includes: [post type](#post-types) (omitted for [Shot](#shot) and [News](#news) types),
[title](#post-title), [description](#post-description) (if any), [author](#post-author) (if the author is not the
[project administrator](#admin)), the name of the requester (if the post was made [by request](#request)),
[tags](#post-tags), [location](#post-location) (if available), and the date of the first publication (for reposts).

[Published posts](https://mwscr.dehero.site/posts/)  
[Published extras](https://mwscr.dehero.site/extras/)  
[Shortlist](https://mwscr.dehero.site/drafts/?publishable=true)

<a id="post-selection"></a>

### Post selection

New posts are selected automatically from the [Drafts](#drafts) that have passed [editing](#editing), according to
scenarios of different [post types](#post-types). A [Posts](#posts) publication is made after 18:00 UTC, and an
[Extras](#extras) publication after 9:00 UTC.

To keep the feed varied, the selection maintains a minimum distance: the same content, post types and related
[locations](#post-location) must be separated by a certain number of other posts. The [editor's mark](#post-mark) is
also taken into account: works with lower marks are published more rarely.

<a id="post-statistics"></a>

### Post statistics

For every publication of a [post](#publication) in the project accounts, the project collects the number of account
followers at the moment of publishing, likes, views, reposts, and comments with replies. These values are refreshed for
a while after publishing: during the first day every 15 minutes, during the first week daily, and later weekly.

From the collected values the project derives the post [rating](#post-rating) and engagement, as well as the total
number of likes, views and comments. The statistics are shown on the [Posts](#posts) and [Extras](#extras) pages and on
each post page, and they also define the [contribution](#members) of members to the project.

<a id="post-rating"></a>

### Post rating

Post rating is calculated for published posts as the average engagement across all [Morrowind Screenshots](#mwscr)
accounts where the post was ever published.

```txt
reactions  = likes + reposts
engagement = reactions / followers x 100   (followers >= 50)
engagement = reactions                     (followers < 50)
rating     = average(engagement)           (2 or more accounts)
```

Accounts with fewer than 50 followers are counted in absolute reactions instead of a percentage. The rating is shown
only when there are engagement values from at least two accounts.

<a id="contribution"></a>

### Members contribution

The contribution of [members](#members) is calculated from their activity in all project sections and channels. For
every member the project counts how many works they [authored](#post-author), how many [locations](#post-location) they
suggested, how many works they [requested](#request) and how many comments they left.

Contribution sorting compares these counts in the order: authored, located, requested, commented, and the sections in
the order: Posts, Extras, Drafts, Rejects. Members with equal counts are ordered by their ID.
