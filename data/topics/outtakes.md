# Outtakes

A set of 10 interesting screenshots or videos from [Morrowind](./morrowind.md) that violate the
[strict rules of the project](./violations.md), submitted by [project members](./members.md). It is the most democratic
[post type](./post-types.md), easy to get into by simply [uploading your screenshots to the project](./proposal.md).

[Go to Outtakes](/extras/?type=outtakes)
