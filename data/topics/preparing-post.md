# preparing a post

Preparing a post for publication includes selecting [type](./post-types.md) and [media](./post-media.md),
[title](./post-title.md) and [description](./post-description.md), [author](./post-author.md), filling in
[tags](./post-tags.md), [game engine](./game-engine-and-utilities.md), [addon](./allowed-mods.md), shooting
[location](./post-location.md) and [placement](./post-placement.md), and selecting [editor's mark](./post-mark.md). The
post [identifier](./post-id.md) is assigned automatically.

---

# подготовка поста

Подготовка поста включает выбор [типа](./post-types.md) и [медиа](./post-media.md), [названия](./post-title.md) и
[описания](./post-description.md), [автора](./post-author.md), заполнение [тегов](./post-tags.md),
[игрового движка](./game-engine-and-utilities.md), [дополнения](./allowed-mods.md), места [съёмки](./post-location.md) и
[размещения](./post-placement.md), а также выбор [метки редактора](./post-mark.md). [Идентификатор поста](./post-id.md)
присваивается автоматически.
